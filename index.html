Our Story

About Sahil Palace
& Restaurant

Nestled in the heart of Sangaria, Rajasthan, Sahil Palace and Restaurant is your home away from home. We believe that great hospitality should be accessible to everyone — which is why we offer premium comfort at truly affordable prices.

With 6 well-appointed rooms ranging from ₹1000 to ₹3000 per night, our hotel caters to families, business travellers, and leisure guests alike. Our in-house restaurant serves a wide spread of authentic Indian cuisine — both vegetarian and non-vegetarian — freshly prepared to delight your taste buds.

We take pride in our warm hospitality, clean spaces, and round-the-clock service. Every guest who walks through our doors is treated like family.
